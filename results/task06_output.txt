TCP + TLS 1.3: old 4-tuple path breaks; reconnect + TCP/TLS setup typically costs about 2 RTT = 200 ms, excluding detection.
QUIC: connection ID can identify the connection on the new path; path validation may cost about 0-1 RTT = 0-100 ms, without a full new TLS handshake.
0-RTT replay warning: early data can be replayed. Do not send money transfers or other non-idempotent state-changing operations as 0-RTT.
QUIC uses UDP; if blocked by a firewall, the client should fall back to TCP-based HTTPS (HTTP/2) where supported.
